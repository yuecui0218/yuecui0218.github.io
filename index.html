About

Food structure, cells, and digestion

My research focuses on how the microscopic structure of plant-based foods influences macronutrient digestion. I am particularly interested in the heterogeneity of individual cotyledon cells and in developing quantitative approaches that connect cellular properties with digestive functionality.

Beyond research, I enjoy learning new things, drawing, reading, yoga, tennis, and diving.

Research

Current research themes

01

Individual cotyledon cells

Characterizing cell-to-cell heterogeneity in morphology and structural properties of pulse cotyledon cells.

02

Structure–digestion relationships

Investigating how cell walls, intracellular matrices, and starch encapsulation contribute to attenuated amylolysis.

03

Quantitative microscopy

Developing microscopy-coupled image analysis workflows for quantitative characterization of cellular properties.
