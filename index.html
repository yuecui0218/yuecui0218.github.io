Research life

Life in and around the lab

My PhD focuses on structure–function relationships in pulses. I work with individual cotyledon cells to understand cellular heterogeneity, quantify structural properties, and explore how food structure influences starch digestion.

Individual cotyledon cells

Structure × digestion
